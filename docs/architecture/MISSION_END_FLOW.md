# End of mission, second dispatch and crew emergency

Contract of the flow after the last patient, and of the flow when a crew member is hurt. Runtime behavior is PENDING in the simulator; `tools/test-end-of-mission-flow.js` proves command order and branching only.

The mission has four distinct places: the starting point (boarding, dispatch), the accident or rescue location (`accident_location`, or `userA` for a transfer), the hospital (`hospital_user`, the destination of the injured) and the base (`RTB_location`). The crew emergency keeps them apart: the hospital never uses the base scene, the base never uses `hospital_user`.

## No automatic end

- `MISSION_END_WAITING` has three values: 0 flying, 1 end menu available, 2 shift ended. Only the END SHIFT button of `end menu` writes 2; `tools/test-end-of-mission-flow.js` rejects any other writer.
- `return to base` no longer raises 1 on a timer. After the landing at the base it starts `deboarding` in a thread and opens the end menu at once: the user may shut down and deboard, or end the shift there (the mission is already counted as completed by `Return to base or continue menu`). `deboarding` raises `MISSION_END_WAITING` to 1 at its end only when it is still 0, so it never overwrites END SHIFT. The pilot leaves when the user opens the pilot door (`SDK_DOOR_COCKPIT_R`), the copilot when the rotor is below 30 and the battery is off.
- When `crew base scene need` is yes (a crew emergency happened, or a crew member aboard is hurt) `return to base` runs `crew casualty destination scene` instead of `deboarding`.

## End menu

`end menu` (04-dispatch-tablet-ui) offers SUBMIT REPORT (legacy), END SHIFT (dispatch phase 7, `MISSION_END_WAITING` 2) and RELOAD MISSION. END SHIFT and RELOAD first set the local `archive_event` (`end_shift`, `reload_mission`) and call `archive closing dispatch`, which writes the automatic snapshot and appends the closing dispatch to `Debug_Dispatch_Table`. RELOAD also sets `dispatch_diag_keep`, so `start location diagnostics` does not clear the dispatch file at the next start. At every start (and every new dispatch preview) `start location diagnostics` first runs `copy automatic snapshot to previous mission`: the whole automatic snapshot file (49 keys) is copied into `Debug_Prev_Table` (`R&E_debug_previous_mission_snapshots`) when it holds a snapshot (`valid` yes), then the automatic file is wiped as before. The previous mission file therefore survives END SHIFT, RELOAD and a mission that never ended, and is replaced only by the next mission snapshot; `prev_copy_missing` reports keys that were not copied.

## Heli rescuer aboard

The end of the mission never comes with a heli rescuer still aboard (tester report 2026-10-17: the patient was unloaded at the hospital and the mission offered END MISSION and RECEIVE NEW DISPATCH while the heli rescuer was in the cabin). `heli rescuer aboard gate` (09-hoist-ground-ops) runs, while `HELIRESCUER_BOARDED` is not 0:

- at the hospital: first thing of `Return to base or continue menu` (after the crew emergency branch, before the modals). Modal "Heli rescuer aboard": DROP HELI RESCUER HERE (the ground deboarding of the original action, `helirescuers_drop_here_mode` hospital_user, hospital or base by the place; it waits for the landing), GO BACK TO THE LOADING POINT (the original GO BACK TO MEETING POINT: FPL 5, `heli_rescuer_location`, where the thread of `drop heli rescuer` drops them; afterwards the route goes on to the base, `PREVIOUS_FPL` 6) and RETURN TO HOME BASE (block 046; the macro returns `base` and the menu ends, the heli rescuer(s) stay aboard);
- at the base: in `return to base` after the landing, before the base scene and the end menu; the modal has DROP HELI RESCUER HERE (BASE) and GO BACK TO THE LOADING POINT.

The macro returns when nobody is aboard. Then the normal modal of the place follows (Mission accomplished: END MISSION, RECEIVE NEW DISPATCH, RETURN TO HOME BASE; at the base the end menu with END SHIFT). A drop made meanwhile with the original action DROP HELIRESCUER HERE closes the modal. `tools/test-heli-rescuer-gate.js` proves the order and the branches; runtime PENDING.

## New dispatch while ending

The dispatch loop (`ready dispatch`) is open from phase 5 (return to base) and 6 (deboarding) while the tablet is connected, and `accept_dispatch` reloads the mission (second dispatch). Before the reload `crew reboard snapshot` stores, in `L:REBOARD_*` (LAT/LON and an ON flag), the position of every crew actor that exists (`pax1`, `pax2`, `pax3`, `copilott`, `pilot`). After the reload `crew boarding` starts with `crew reboard interrupted crew`: the seat is released, the actor is created at the stored position, walks to its door with the deboarding states (pax3: 16/14 with three crew, 3/1 with four or five), the door opens, the actor is destroyed, the seat trigger and payload station are restored. The flags are cleared by `crew reboard clear` (every first dispatch and after use). Crew that had already finished (actors destroyed) are boarded by the existing start sequence: `boarding` with the car when `BACK_TO_HOSPITAL`, otherwise `crew boarding` on foot.

No dispatch is loaded or accepted during a crew emergency: `next_dispatch`, `ready dispatch` and `accept_dispatch` check `CREW_EMERGENCY_ACTIVE`, which `initialize crew lifescores for shift` resets at every mission start together with the other emergency locals (it also clears `MISSION_FAILED`, which would otherwise survive `reload_mission` and label the next mission as failed).

## Crew emergency

LifeScore thresholds (locals set in `initialize crew lifescores for shift`, changeable in one place): a member at 10 or less is critical and starts the emergency (`crew impact events`), 0 is dead. `CREW_AMBULANCE_LIMIT` (10) is the LifeScore up to which a member aboard is a casualty (the ambulance comes at the base, the hospital unloads the member), `CREW_POLICE_LIMIT` (20) the one up to which a member aboard is injured (the police waits at the base).

1. `crew emergency response` runs once per mission for the first event. A death calls `crew emergency divert` at once. A critical member calls `crew emergency choice`: the modal CREW EMERGENCY offers Divert to hospital (`crew emergency divert`) or Continue mission (`crew emergency continue`, `CREW_CONTINUE_CHOSEN`). After Continue a further critical member does not ask again; a death diverts (`CREW_DIVERT` no, `CREW_EMERGENCY_ACTIVE` yes). `MISSION_FAILED` is `CREW_FATAL` or `CREW_CRITICAL`. With a patient aboard (`L:HOISTED` 1, `L:SAVED` not 1) there is no question: the helicopter goes to a hospital whatever the answer and a critical member is handed over there anyway, so the response calls `crew emergency continue` directly. Before the pick up (Divert cancels the mission) and after the delivery (divert to a hospital or go on to the base) the question stays.
2. `crew emergency divert` (idempotent, `CREW_DIVERT` yes) cancels the mission: guidance off, `MISSION_PHASE` 8, dispatch phase 7, hover display off. The crew reports the abort to the dispatcher (sender `-OUT-`, see [RESCUETRACK_MESSAGES.md](RESCUETRACK_MESSAGES.md)). A patient not loaded yet (`L:HOISTED` not 1, phase before 8) stays on scene: the crew asks for another unit and the dispatcher answers that it tasks one (random name, `crew emergency replacement unit`; the unit is not simulated). It then starts `crew emergency route to care`, which reports the confirmed hospital. `crew emergency choice` hides the dispatch page before the modal and the question is on screen only.
3. `crew emergency route to care`: the crew boards (`board crew after emergency`), the hospital of the dispatch (`hospital_user`; `Query closest hospital` only when missing) is proposed by `crew emergency destination menu` (modal CHOOSE DESTINATION: Accept selection, or Manually select destination with `Choose manual type modal`; the shared `user destination menu`, where the manual selection ends, diverts to it while `CREW_DIVERT` is yes). `CREW_DEST_CONFIRMED` yes continues. Without any hospital the user selects one manually; there is no return to base.
4. With a patient aboard (`L:HOISTED` 1, `L:SAVED` not 1) the normal destination flow runs from the confirmed hospital (`manual_hospital_destination_setup`): route, unloading, `ambulance_prime` button and `FORCE_AMBULANCE` as for any patient. Its end (`Return to base or continue menu`) calls `crew hospital finish` when `CREW_EMERGENCY_ACTIVE` is yes and the destination is not an ambulance meeting point (`cambuchoice` not yes): the critical crew still aboard is handed over, then the return to base. This also hands over a critical member when the user chose Continue, because a hospital stop forces the unloading. The patient unloading sends the medic and the copilot out of their seats (`CREW_OUT_n`) and the flow ends right after their boarding trigger, so `crew hospital finish` first calls `crew hospital crew back`: it ticks the seat tracker until every member up to `CREW_MEMBER_CAP` is back in a seat (30 s at most). Without it `crew casualty plan` saw the critical member as not aboard and nobody was handed over (169.27 tester run). The wait is a safeguard for the members who are fit: the primary rule is that a casualty never walks in the patient flow and is unloaded in parallel with the patient (see Hospital unload). A member already handed over (`CCS_DONE_n`) is not waited for.
5. Without a patient: `crew emergency ambulance prime` and `crew hospital arrival`. The ambulance follows the patient rules: with `FORCE_AMBULANCE` no and a hospital (`POI_TYPE` 2) the user asks for it with the button Unload to ambulance (`crew_ambulance_prime`), shown when the destination is confirmed and destroyed at 0.2 NM or when selected; otherwise it comes by itself (`crew hospital ambulance call`, `L:AMBULANCE` 2). No ambulance and no police car are parked at the hospital for the crew. Arrival: distance 0.25 NM, on the ground, speed 1 kt, rotor below 10 (`GNDOPS_ROTOR` 1) or 81, then the unloading and `crew emergency return to base`.
6. A dead crew member aboard is met by the police at the hospital: `crew hospital police watch` (a thread from the confirmation, acting at 2 NM) calls `deceased patient police scene`, the same one of a dead patient.

### Hospital unload

`crew hospital unload` hands over the casualties (aboard, not handed over, LifeScore at or below `CREW_AMBULANCE_LIMIT`), worst first, after the patient. Each casualty k (1 to 4) has its own macros with literal names: `chs_str_k` (Medic Stretcher, VAR 2 1 empty, 3 carried), places and routes of its own (`crew hospital unload k`, `crew hospital deliver walk k`, `crew hospital deliver ambulance k`). The medic: when one exists (the medic of the patient, `ambumedic`) he is enough and nothing is created; when none exists (a crew alone, or the medic of the patient already gone) one medic `chs_med_1` is created at the hospital, once. He goes to the helicopter with the first stretcher, toward the casualty, but ends slightly aside so that he does not overlap the stretcher (`chs_mdoor_1`: 15 degrees toward the nose from the door and 5.5 m from the helicopter, the stretcher comes from abeam to 4 m) facing the door, and waits there; with a single casualty he comes back with the stretcher, with several he follows the last one (`CHS_TOTAL`), along the same hospital waypoints. At `hospital_door` stretchers and medic are removed: arrived, nobody goes anywhere else (169.29 test: a medic per casualty walked to the hospital on his own and to a point behind the door while the stretcher was still loading, and the stretchers had an end point behind the door). With the ambulance the medic goes to its side with the last stretcher and is removed when it is back.

- With a patient (`User destination1` and `User destination2`, once the rotor has stopped and before the ground team moves) `crew hospital scene stretchers` runs the unload in parallel with the patient: `crew casualty plan`, `crew hospital handover roles`, then, with casualties, `CHS_PARALLEL` yes and `crew hospital scene place`. A realistic handover does not send a critically injured medic to hand over the patient and then bring him back: the member who hands over the patient (`hoist_crew`, member 2, member 5 with five crew) and the member of the paperwork (member 4, the copilot with three crew) stay in their seats when they are casualties (`CHS_MEDIC_HURT`, `CHS_PAPER_HURT`): the seat triggers around `hoist_crew` are skipped (the actor is then hospital staff, same crew model) and the paperwork is skipped. The ground team of the patient (`ambumedic`, `ambustretcher`) first points at `hospital_user`; one empty `Airbus H145 Medic Stretcher` per casualty (`chs_str_1..4`, no extra medic) is placed beside it with `bearing2` 270 (left of `ambustretcher`) and 90 (right of `ambumedic`) at 3 m, the third and fourth at 6 m, and points at `hospital_user`. `crew hospital unload` then runs in its own thread: the casualties are loaded from the seats while the patient is handed over, each stretcher leaves for the hospital door (or the ambulance, one casualty at a time in order, `CHS_AMB_TURN`) only after the patient (`L:SAVED` 1). `crew hospital finish` waits for `CHS_UNLOADED`. Without casualties, or without this scene (other destination flows, crew alone), the unload runs after the patient or alone with its own medic and stretcher as before.
- Loading is the one of the base scene: the casualty door opens, the stretcher goes to 4 m from the helicopter at the door (around the nose when it is on the other side), the seat is released, the person appears on the stretcher, the door closes. A dead member is carried like the others and never walks.
- Without the ambulance the stretcher leaves from the door side and follows `hospital_wp1..3` (the custom hospital waypoints when `hospital_wptot` is 1 to 3) to `hospital_door` and to its own end point (`chs_end_k`, bearing 0, 90, 180, 270 from the hospital); the medic follows to its own point. The next casualty is loaded while the previous one walks. Both objects are removed there.
- With the ambulance (`L:AMBULANCE` 1 or 2; after a patient the ambulance of the patient is waited for and called again) the stretcher goes to its rear (`bearing2` 180) and is removed, the ambulance drives to `hospital_door` and back to `DAMBU`, one casualty at a time.
- `CCS_DONE_n` marks the members handed over (the plan ignores them), `CHS_UNLOADED` releases the police guard.

### Dispatch page: crew lifescores and statistics of the old dispatch

- The crew rows of the dispatch page (`04-dispatch-tablet-ui`) are listed in the order Pilot, Copilot, Medic, HEMS 1, HEMS 2 (members 1, 3, 2, 4, 5). Every member has five rows: the normal row (shown with Show lifescores, only while the score is above `CREW_AMBULANCE_LIMIT`), a red `- CRITICAL` row (score above 0 and at or below the limit), a red `- DECEASED` row (0), and, once the member is unloaded at the hospital, a red `- UNLOADED AT HOSPITAL` or `- DECEASED, UNLOADED AT HOSPITAL` row that replaces the critical or deceased one. The red rows ignore the show or hide choice and the mission phase: a critical member is always visible. Renderer conditions are AND only.
- `CREW_HOSPITAL_n` (n = member 1..5, cleared by `initialize crew lifescores for shift`) is set to 1 by `crew hospital deliver walk k` when the stretcher k has reached `hospital_door`, and by `crew hospital deliver ambulance k` when the ambulance with the casualty has reached `hospital_door`; the member is found through `CHS_MEMBER_k`.
- The statistics of the old dispatch (`DISPATCH_ENDED` 1) are L: simulator variables (`L:STAT_TTG` time to the scene, written by `ground ops` when the rotor is stopped on the scene, `L:STAT_TOG` end of the on-site operations, `L:STAT_TOT` arrival at the hospital, `L:OLD_HELOVICTIMS`): `reset mission statistics` clears them at every mission start (`delayed threads`) and every row is shown only when its value was set. In a mission diverted by a crew emergency the on-scene `ground ops` is still armed (nothing cancels it) and passed its rotor gate when the rotor was stopped at the hospital, writing `STAT_TTG` 660 s (169.29 test, the 11:00 of the page): it writes nothing while `CREW_DIVERT` is yes (time to the scene above 0, on-site time only when `STAT_TOG` is above `STAT_TTG`, total time only when `STAT_TOT` is above 0). `Scene not reached` is shown when the scene was not. The crew alone sets `STAT_TOT` at the hospital landing (`crew hospital arrival`).
- Failure statistics (`MISSION_FAILED` set): `mission failure statistics`, called by `return to base menu` when the dispatch ends, counts the crew members seriously injured (above 0, at or below `CREW_AMBULANCE_LIMIT`), the deceased and the patients deceased (only when the scene was reached); the page shows the result (`MISSION FAILED: crew member seriously injured` or `deceased`), the cause (`CREW_EMERGENCY_CAUSE`), the counts and the patients deceased when there are any.

### Return to base and base scene

`crew emergency return to base` calls `return to base menu`: the pilot who remains flies back. `crew base scene need` (when the base is approached and at the landing) decides the base scene: yes after a crew emergency, or when somebody aboard is hurt (LifeScore at or below `CREW_POLICE_LIMIT`), also without an emergency. The police is always there in that case; the ambulance, its medic and stretcher only for a casualty aboard (a member who was not handed over, for instance with Continue and no hospital stop).

- `crew casualty scene vehicles` (idempotent, `ccs_vehicles_ready` no/working/yes, started 2 NM out): `police7` and `ccs_policeman` 50 m from `ccs_site` (`RTB_location`) at bearing 340; for a casualty aboard also `ambulance5` at bearing 20 (34 m apart, heading away so that its rear faces the site), `ccs_medic` at its left side (`bearing2` 270, 3 m) and `ccs_stretcher` behind it (`bearing2` 180, 5 m, VAR 2 1).
- `crew casualty plan`: casualty count, injured count, dead count, `CCS_LOAD_MEMBER` (lowest score, lowest index on a tie), `CCS_WALK_MEMBER` (the pilot when alive, aboard, not loaded, otherwise the copilot).
- `crew casualty destination scene`: wait for rotor below 30; the policeman (and the medic for a casualty) walk to the stage point of the side closest to each and face the helicopter; the worst casualty is loaded (`crew casualty load member`); with a single casualty or none the pilot walks (`crew casualty pilot walk`); then `MISSION_END_WAITING` 1, `end menu`, dialog.
- Pilot walk: the pilot door is opened by the user (message and gate as in `deboarding`), the copilot door by the scene; the person walks directly to 2.5 m in front of the policeman when both are on the same side, otherwise around the nose; the policeman points at the person. Two or more casualties aboard: one is loaded, nobody walks.

## Deceased patient aboard

The patient is always unloaded at a hospital, never at the base, with the normal destination choreography (stretchers, ambulance only when present or selected): it frees the helipad and is not changed. `deceased patient police scene` parks `police7` and `ccs_policeman` 50 m from `hospital_user` when `patientlife` is `died` at 2 NM from the destination (these vehicles are independent of the hospital ambulance). `deceased patient police guard`: after the unloading (`L:SAVED` 1, or `CHS_UNLOADED` yes for the crew), at the first sign of departure (a fuel prime pump ON, or NR above 5 with `GNDOPS_ROTOR` 1, above 86 with 0) the policeman walks to 12 m from the helicopter at the bearing 300 or 30 closest to him and faces the user.

## Documented states used

`Airbus H145 Medic` and `Policeman`: VAR 1 0, walking only through `drive_object` VAR1 1. `Medic Stretcher`: VAR 1 0, walking through VAR1 1, VAR 2 1 empty and 3 carried load. `$TITLE Crew`: pilots VAR 1 14/16 with VAR 2 1 (pilot) and 3 (copilot); rear crew 0/2 (pax1, pax2) and 3/1 (pax3 with four or five crew).
