# Voice app mode (HPG Mission Companion)

The mission already had a mode for an external audio program: `L:AUDIOINHIBIT` not 0 makes `VCP` equal to `APP` (macro `mission start` area of `11-mission-lifecycle.json` and the thread `EXTERNAL XML AUDIO ENABLE` in `threads/01-startup-and-dispatch-triggers.json`), so every voice and sound call `L:<role>{local:VCP}<n> += 1` increments `L:<role>APP<n>`, a counter that no `sound.xml` of the wav packs knows: the aircraft plays nothing and the external program reacts to the counter. The HPG Mission Companion (repository `HPG-Mission-Companion`, version 0.5 or later) is that program: it sets `L:AUDIOINHIBIT` to 1, reads the counters through SimConnect and speaks the matching message of its voice pack EN (`voicepacks/EN.xml`: key `ROLE.NUMBER` = LVAR `L:ROLE{VCP}NUMBER`). Sounds without voice (ringtones `L:{VCP}DISPATCHED..4`, `DISPATCH2`, `DEAD`) are played by the program as wav files of its audio folder. The new message tone (`L:{VCP}NEWMSG`) is not in the pack on purpose: the tablet plays its own sound, which has no `VCP` in its name and is never inhibited, so playing it too would double it. The call sites of the mission did not change.

The conversations run in their own thread, only with `VCP` = `APP` (a wav pack has no recording of them) and under the same `AUDIO_DECLUT` rule as the other crew chatter. The lines are 0.4 s apart so that the program sees the counters in order and speaks them one after the other.

Other changes: the checklists are untouched (377 plain `speak` texts in `22-checklists.json`); when `VCP` is `APP` the connection of the voice server (`checklist home` and `voice app connect`) sends `<defaultactor:11>`, a state tag of the program: from then on every text without an actor tag is spoken by actor 11, the checklist voice. The tag is sent only with `VCP` = `APP` because the old audio service would read it aloud. `11-mission-lifecycle.json` `cancel HEMS dispatch`: `L:PLT{VCP}46` (dispatch cancelled). `10-transfer-special-missions.json`: `L:PAX3{VCP}36` when `ops_progress_warning` becomes 1.

## Context published for the radio rule

`L:HMC_HELI_OFF` is 1 when `L:{HXX}_SDK_OH_BATTERY_MASTER` is 0 and `L:{HXX}_SDK_ROTOR_RPM` is below 1. `L:HMC_CREW_PRESENT` has bit 0 for the pilot (`L:{HXX}_IMPL_PILOT0` 0), 1 the copilot (member 3), 2 the doctor (member 2), 3 HEMS1 (member 4), 4 HEMS2 (member 5), each when its `CREW_OUT_n` is 0 and `CREW_MEMBER_CAP` allows it (the flags of `crew presence flags`). The tracker loop runs while `CREW_LIFESCORE_MONITOR_ACTIVE` is "yes"; before that the context is not valid and the program adds no radio tag. The rule is in the program (`VOICEPACKS.md` there): helicopter off voice, dispatcher radio, inside and outside radio, everybody outside voice.

`data` `technicians` (`03-people-and-names.json`) lists the two technician models: index 0 male (`Airbus H145 Engineer 1`, 30West `sam 5`), index 1 female (`Airbus H145 Engineer 2`, 30West `Eve`). The macro `technician pick` (`03-aircraft-crew-checklists.json`, parameter `scene` `boarding` or `arrival`) sets the local `TECH_SEX` and, with `VCP` = `APP`, picks it at random (`floor(rand[0,2])`) and writes `L:HMC_TECH_SEX` (0 male, 1 female); with the wav voice packs it stays fixed (boarding male = Frank, arrival female = Sarah), so the wav voice always fits the model. `technician title` returns the object title of `technicians[sex]` (the 30West model only with the easter egg `fool` and 30West installed, unless `classic` is 1). Used by `boarding` (`engineera`), `return to base` (`engineer`) and `fuel truck` (`truckerf`: with `VCP` = `APP` it follows `TECH_SEX`; otherwise as before, female when the boarding technician is already there). The counters `L:FRA{VCP}N` and `L:SARA{VCP}N` are unchanged; the Companion (0.8, pack EN 4) reads `L:HMC_TECH_SEX` and speaks the options of that technician with actor 6 or 7. ## Who can speak, and small talk (sources only; pack EN 6)

- `voice context publish` also writes `L:HMC_CREW_MUTE` (bit 0 pilot, 1 copilot, 2 doctor, 3 HEMS1, 4 HEMS2, as `L:HMC_CREW_PRESENT`): a bit is set when the member is dead (`CREW_DEAD_n` 1) or has `CREW_LIFESCORE_n` under 15 (member 1 pilot, 2 doctor, 3 copilot, 4 HEMS1, 5 HEMS2). The Companion keeps the lines of that actor silent, whatever macro or counter asked for them; there is no replacement speaker.
- `voice calm` (params `patient`, `casualties`): 1 when there is no crew emergency or mission failure, every crew member of the flight is alive with `CREW_LIFESCORE_n` of 85 or more, and, with `patient` 1, the patient is not dying (`LIFESCORE` under 50), dead (`patientlife` died) or in CPR (`L:CPR`), and, with `casualties` 1, nobody died in the session (`PATIENTS_KILLED_TOTAL`).
- `voice banter`: one of three small talk conversations (yesterday BAN.Y, a match BAN.M, company BAN.C; four lines each after 5 to 15 s) when `voice calm` allows it. `voice cruise chat` calls it, seven times in ten, after the chat of each long leg (to the scene, to the hospital, back to the base: route 6, more than 7 km from RTB_location); the chats CRZ.* need `voice calm` too. The messages are futile ones of the pack (options 1 and 2 silent), so often nothing is said.

## News, not a timer; dynamic briefing; crew chatter (sources only; pack EN 7)

- News, not a timer (`voice ground update`): the monitor computes a key for the stage and a bucket of the minutes left (0, 1, then every two minutes). The team speaks when that signature changes and at least 20 s passed since the last report (stage, who transports and the time left, then the pilot acknowledges with GRD.PLT.OK). Without news: after 240 s and with the pilot alone (`L:HMC_CREW_PRESENT` 1 or less, nobody else aboard) the pilot asks for the status (GRD.PLT.Q) and the usual answer follows; after 150 s of silence with somebody aboard they make small talk (`voice banter`, which asks `voice calm`). A dead patient (`patientlife` died) is never described as stable or critical: the doctor says GRD.DEAD (death confirmed, documentation, coming back), loading becomes the crew return, and nobody says who transports. The thread works on params copied from the locals the monitor rewrites every 2 s.
- Dynamic briefing (`voice briefing chat` starts with `voice dispatch exchange`, param `cls` = the mission class): the pilot asks the dispatcher for an update by radio (DSP.RQ) and the dispatcher answers with what is useful for the kind of mission (DSP.ANS.ACC, INJ, SAR for the four searches, TRF, ORG); with CREW CHATTER on FULL and in four dispatches out of ten the crew presents itself (BRF.INT.PLT, CPL, DOC, CHF; futile lines, no names, so that they sound right with the job words too). The existing crew briefing follows.
- Where the crew is left (`voice approach briefing`, two NM from the accident): plan P when the landing spot was chosen (`customLZ` yes), the police is on scene or on the way (`POLICE_AVAIL` 1 or more, `policeloadingenabled` not no) and the patient is farther than `police_bring_crew_min_dist` (200 m) from the landing spot, whatever the ground or hoist plan: the pilot says the distance (meters, rounded to 50, argument of APR.PLT.P) and that the police drives the crew; the chief answers with APR.CHF.P. The ground and hoist plans G and H are unchanged.
- Setting CREW CHATTER (`global:CREW_CHATTER`, `(P)` option of the audio tab of the settings, saved in the profiles and cleared by resetdefault; null = FULL): 0 FULL, 1 REDUCED, 2 MINIMAL. `voice context publish` writes `L:HMC_CHAT_MAX` (6, 3 or 1) and includes it in the change signature. The Companion never picks an option number above it (`Pick`): option 1 is the default wording, 2 and 3 professional, 4 to 6 colloquial, and a futile message has two silent options first and its default as the third, so REDUCED (3) lets a futile line through only at its default wording (one time in three) and uses the professional wording elsewhere, and MINIMAL (1) uses the default wording everywhere and keeps the futile lines silent. The mission also stops what only the Companion could mute: `voice calm` returns 0 with REDUCED or MINIMAL (no cruise chat, no banter, no ground small talk) and the crew presentation is skipped. The Companion without the variable (0.8 and before) uses all six options.
