# Voice app mode (HPG Mission Companion)

The mission already had a mode for an external audio program: `L:AUDIOINHIBIT` not 0 makes `VCP` equal to `APP` (macro `mission start` area of `11-mission-lifecycle.json` and the thread `EXTERNAL XML AUDIO ENABLE` in `threads/01-startup-and-dispatch-triggers.json`), so every voice and sound call `L:<role>{local:VCP}<n> += 1` increments `L:<role>APP<n>`, a counter that no `sound.xml` of the wav packs knows: the aircraft plays nothing and the external program reacts to the counter. The HPG Mission Companion (repository `HPG-Mission-Companion`, version 0.5 or later) is that program: it sets `L:AUDIOINHIBIT` to 1, reads the counters through SimConnect and speaks the matching message of its voice pack EN (`voicepacks/EN.xml`: key `ROLE.NUMBER` = LVAR `L:ROLE{VCP}NUMBER`). Sounds without voice (ringtones `L:{VCP}DISPATCHED..4`, `DISPATCH2`, `DEAD`) are played by the program as wav files of its audio folder. The new message tone (`L:{VCP}NEWMSG`) is not in the pack on purpose: the tablet plays its own sound, which has no `VCP` in its name and is never inhibited, so playing it too would double it. The call sites of the mission did not change.

The conversations run in their own thread, only with `VCP` = `APP` (a wav pack has no recording of them) and under the same `AUDIO_DECLUT` rule as the other crew chatter. The lines are 0.4 s apart so that the program sees the counters in order and speaks them one after the other.

Other changes: the checklists are untouched (377 plain `speak` texts in `22-checklists.json`); when `VCP` is `APP` the connection of the voice server (`checklist home` and `voice app connect`) sends `<defaultactor:11>`, a state tag of the program: from then on every text without an actor tag is spoken by actor 11, the checklist voice. The tag is sent only with `VCP` = `APP` because the old audio service would read it aloud. `11-mission-lifecycle.json` `cancel HEMS dispatch`: `L:PLT{VCP}46` (dispatch cancelled). `10-transfer-special-missions.json`: `L:PAX3{VCP}36` when `ops_progress_warning` becomes 1.

## Context published for the radio rule

`L:HMC_HELI_OFF` is 1 when `L:{HXX}_SDK_OH_BATTERY_MASTER` is 0 and `L:{HXX}_SDK_ROTOR_RPM` is below 1. `L:HMC_CREW_PRESENT` has bit 0 for the pilot (`L:{HXX}_IMPL_PILOT0` 0), 1 the copilot (member 3), 2 the doctor (member 2), 3 HEMS1 (member 4), 4 HEMS2 (member 5), each when its `CREW_OUT_n` is 0 and `CREW_MEMBER_CAP` allows it (the flags of `crew presence flags`). The tracker loop runs while `CREW_LIFESCORE_MONITOR_ACTIVE` is "yes"; before that the context is not valid and the program adds no radio tag. The rule is in the program (`VOICEPACKS.md` there, section "Context for the radio rule"; the user's rules of 2026-10-11): the dispatcher is an external radio call heard with the battery on or the helicopter at the base; the crew has radios (radio when somebody is outside, intercom with the battery on and voice with the battery off when everybody is aboard); the technician, the ground unit and the hospital staff speak by voice, heard from inside up to 30 m, very low up to 100 m, not beyond; the technician next to the helicopter with the pilot aboard and the battery on uses the intercom.

The same macro also publishes `L:HMC_CREW_EXIST` (the bits of `L:HMC_CREW_PRESENT` for the members that exist, from `CREW_MEMBER_CAP`: who may be outside), `L:HMC_AT_BASE` (1 while the local `wifiinrange` is "in": the helicopter is within 300 m of the start base, the dispatcher reaches the crew radios even with the battery off), `L:HMC_ZONE_TECH` (nearest of `engineera`, `engineer`) and `L:HMC_ZONE_GROUND` (nearest of `marshall`, `ambumedic`, `ambumedic3`): 0 no such object, 1 up to 5 m, 2 up to 30 m, 3 up to 100 m, 4 beyond, measured from the user aircraft by `voice zone nearest` and `voice zone class` (the distance query is in a try, an error never stops the seat tracker loop). The battery master is not published: the program reads `L:H145_SDK_OH_BATTERY_MASTER`. No patient zone is published: no pack message has the patient as speaker.

The hospital dialog (`voice hospital handover`, HOS.WEL, HOS.HND, HOS.THX or HOS.ORG) starts when the crew meets the ground crew in each of the five unloading variants in `05-navigation-queries.json` (the crew reaches the stretcher team, or the stretcher team reaches the helicopter), before the tablet message Patient handed over to ED; it was started after that message, with the patient already unloaded.

## Technicians (random male or female, in sources only)

`data` `technicians` (`03-people-and-names.json`) lists the two technician models: index 0 male (`Airbus H145 Engineer 1`, 30West `sam 5`), index 1 female (`Airbus H145 Engineer 2`, 30West `Eve`). The macro `technician pick` (`03-aircraft-crew-checklists.json`, parameter `scene` `boarding` or `arrival`) sets the local `TECH_SEX` and, with `VCP` = `APP`, picks it at random (`floor(rand[0,2])`) and writes `L:HMC_TECH_SEX` (0 male, 1 female); with the wav voice packs it stays fixed (boarding male = Frank, arrival female = Sarah), so the wav voice always fits the model. `technician title` returns the object title of `technicians[sex]` (the 30West model only with the easter egg `fool` and 30West installed, unless `classic` is 1). Used by `boarding` (`engineera`), `return to base` (`engineer`) and `fuel truck` (`truckerf`: with `VCP` = `APP` it follows `TECH_SEX`; otherwise as before, female when the boarding technician is already there). The counters `L:FRA{VCP}N` and `L:SARA{VCP}N` are unchanged; the Companion (0.8, pack EN 4) reads `L:HMC_TECH_SEX` and speaks the options of that technician with actor 6 or 7. The crew lines that greet the technician (CPL.23, CPL.39, PAX1.37, PAX1.38, PAX2.2, PLT.5, PLT.9, PLT.21) use `{@TECH}` and no longer name Frank or Sarah. ## Who can speak, and small talk (sources only; pack EN 6)

- `voice context publish` also writes `L:HMC_CREW_MUTE` (bit 0 pilot, 1 copilot, 2 doctor, 3 HEMS1, 4 HEMS2, as `L:HMC_CREW_PRESENT`): a bit is set when the member is dead (`CREW_DEAD_n` 1) or has `CREW_LIFESCORE_n` under 15 (member 1 pilot, 2 doctor, 3 copilot, 4 HEMS1, 5 HEMS2). The Companion keeps the lines of that actor silent, whatever macro or counter asked for them; there is no replacement speaker for a crew member who exists but cannot speak. A seat that the flight does not have (`L:HMC_CREW_EXIST` bit missing, for example HEMS1 and HEMS2 with 3 crew) is different: from the Companion 0.23 its text messages (pack texts, not wav files) are said by a crew member who is there (tester report 2026-10-14, PAX1 lines with 3 crew). `voice app connect` also sends `<mission:RE>`, the identity of the mission.
- `voice calm` (params `patient`, `casualties`): 1 when there is no crew emergency or mission failure, every crew member of the flight is alive with `CREW_LIFESCORE_n` of 85 or more, and, with `patient` 1, the patient is not dying (`LIFESCORE` under 50), dead (`patientlife` died) or in CPR (`L:CPR`), and, with `casualties` 1, nobody died in the session (`PATIENTS_KILLED_TOTAL`).
- `voice banter`: one of three small talk conversations (yesterday BAN.Y, a match BAN.M, company BAN.C; four lines each after 5 to 15 s) when `voice calm` allows it. `voice cruise chat` calls it, seven times in ten, after the chat of each long leg (to the scene, to the hospital, back to the base: route 6, more than 7 km from RTB_location); the chats CRZ.* need `voice calm` too. The messages are futile ones of the pack (options 1 and 2 silent), so often nothing is said.

## News, not a timer; dynamic briefing; crew chatter (sources only; pack EN 7)

- News, not a timer (`voice ground update`): the monitor computes a key for the stage and a bucket of the minutes left (0, 1, then every two minutes). The team speaks when that signature changes and at least 20 s passed since the last report (stage, who transports and the time left, then the pilot acknowledges with GRD.PLT.OK). Without news: after 240 s and with the pilot alone (`L:HMC_CREW_PRESENT` 1 or less, nobody else aboard) the pilot asks for the status (GRD.PLT.Q) and the usual answer follows; after 150 s of silence with somebody aboard they make small talk (`voice banter`, which asks `voice calm`). A dead patient (`patientlife` died) is never described as stable or critical: the doctor says GRD.DEAD (death confirmed, documentation, coming back), loading becomes the crew return, and nobody says who transports. The thread works on params copied from the locals the monitor rewrites every 2 s.
- Dynamic briefing (`voice briefing chat` starts with `voice dispatch exchange`, param `cls` = the mission class): the pilot asks the dispatcher for an update by radio (DSP.RQ) and the dispatcher answers with what is useful for the kind of mission (DSP.ANS.ACC, INJ, SAR for the four searches, TRF, ORG); with CREW CHATTER on FULL and in four dispatches out of ten the crew presents itself (BRF.INT.PLT, CPL, DOC, CHF; futile lines, no names, so that they sound right with the job words too). The existing crew briefing follows.
- Where the crew is left (`voice approach briefing`, two NM from the accident): plan P when the landing spot was chosen (`customLZ` yes), the police is on scene or on the way (`POLICE_AVAIL` 1 or more, `policeloadingenabled` not no) and the patient is farther than `police_bring_crew_min_dist` (200 m) from the landing spot, whatever the ground or hoist plan: the pilot says the distance (meters, rounded to 50, argument of APR.PLT.P) and that the police drives the crew; the chief answers with APR.CHF.P. The ground and hoist plans G and H are unchanged.
- Setting CREW CHATTER (`global:CREW_CHATTER`, `(P)` option of the audio tab of the settings, saved in the profiles and cleared by resetdefault; null = FULL): 0 FULL, 1 REDUCED, 2 MINIMAL. `voice context publish` writes `L:HMC_CHAT_MAX` (6, 3 or 1) and includes it in the change signature. The Companion never picks an option number above it (`Pick`): option 1 is the default wording, 2 and 3 professional, 4 to 6 colloquial, and a futile message has two silent options first and its default as the third, so REDUCED (3) lets a futile line through only at its default wording (one time in three) and uses the professional wording elsewhere, and MINIMAL (1) uses the default wording everywhere and keeps the futile lines silent. The mission also stops what only the Companion could mute: `voice calm` returns 0 with REDUCED or MINIMAL (no cruise chat, no banter, no ground small talk) and the crew presentation is skipped. The Companion without the variable (0.8 and before) uses all six options.

## Translated packs: the texts with keys (sources only, after 169.47; Companion 0.13, pack EN 9)

Users make translated packs of the Companion. The pack messages (`<say:KEY>`, the counters) were already translatable; two kinds of English text were not: the 377 checklist rows (`22-checklists.json`) and the 288 dispatch descriptions (`description1-4` of the 72 incidents in `02-waypoints-and-categories.json`, spoken by `voice read dispatch`). Both are free texts with a key now: `<text:KEY>English`.

- Checklists: every `speak` of `22-checklists.json` starts with `<text:CHK.<macro>.<nn>>`, for example `CHK.interiorckl.02` (macro name without spaces or symbols, a number that is never reused). The English stays in the macro: it is what a pack without that key speaks, and the source of `tools/voice-texts.json`. A new row: write the `speak`, run `node tools/voice-texts.js assign`.
- Dispatch description: `voice read dispatch` speaks `<actor8><radio><text:DSC.{0}.{1}>{2}` with `VAR_MISSION_NUMBER` (which is `ID_CARD` of the incident), `CRASH_VARIABLE` (the variant 1-4) and the description shown on the tablet. The Companion checks that the English it receives is the one of the key in the base pack; if not (an old key, a reworded description) it speaks the English. The search missions with a vehicle (65-67) append `Vehicle: <colour> <model>.` to the description in `SAR car dispatch text`; that sentence is now read apart (`<text:DSC.VEH>`, values colour and model after `//`), with the base description kept in `sar_desc_base`, so the translated pack speaks the base description and its own `Vehicle:` sentence. The colour and the model stay as they are in the table (English words).
- The list of keys and English texts is `tools/voice-texts.json` (666: 377 + 288 + the vehicle template); `node tools/voice-texts.js check` and `tools/test-voice-texts.js` verify it, and the Companion base pack `voicepacks/EN.xml` holds the same ones (`node tools/sync-pack-texts.js` in its repository). The `<pack:EN:13>` of `voice app connect` is the version of the pack that holds the `<say:KEY>` messages the mission uses (11: CRW.VEH.*, HOSP.*; 12: DSA.*); a `<text:KEY>` needs no pack version, its English is the fallback.
- Not translated by this: the texts on the screen (tablet, buttons, messages), the proper names (places, hospitals, patients), the colour and model of the search vehicles. The voices of a translated pack must be voices of its language (Actors and voices tab of the Companion).

## Existing defects found while reading the call sites (not changed)

- `09-hoist-ground-ops.json` (about lines 2333 and 2339): `L:PAX3{VCP}26` and `25` are set to `L:PAX3{VCP}59` and `55` plus 1; those two variables are never written, so the value is always 1 and the "welcome" and "briefing" lines sound only once per flight load.
- `09-hoist-ground-ops.json`: `L:{VCP}89` ("Personnel 2 to ground") has no sound in the wav packs, and `L:{VCP}86` has three wav variants (`pls.set.hoist.sw.on1..3`).
- Crew tips (pack EN 13, user request of 2026-10-14): the macro `voice tip` (called by `voice cruise chat` in the three long legs) tells a four-line conversation `TIP.<topic>.1` to `4` of the copilot, the doctor and sometimes the pilot about a function of the mission pilots may not know: SAV saves, MSL mission list presets, PRF settings profiles, CRW crew health, KIT kit weights, QLK quick links, DBG debug snapshots, MED medical options, FLR failures, CHT crew chatter, CND the option On conditions missions, TOF how to switch the tips off. The topics follow each other in this order from a random start (the local `vtp_next` survives the reloads). The tips have their own setting CREW TIPS (global `CREW_TIPS`, 0 or empty on, 1 off; Settings, Audio options, buttons TIPS ON and TIPS OFF; kept in the aircraft profiles; Reset to default clears it), independent from CREW CHATTER: `voice calm` is called with the param `tip` 1 and does not stop on the chatter setting, but still refuses with a crew emergency, a hurt crew member, a dying patient or a death. The messages are not futile (six options, none silent): with CREW CHATTER REDUCED or MINIMAL the Companion limits the option number, and every option tells the whole information. Wav packs say nothing (`voice say` needs the Companion). Test: `node tools/test-voice-tips.js`.
